## Context

F-8 shows per screen and period: people in view, vehicles (about half of screens, D-10), screen looks, look share, age groups 18–24, 25–34, 35–44, 45–54, 55+, gender «предположительно», and a chart per hour. F-2 needs estimated contacts (D-4) and looks; F-3 needs approximate audience per screen for the period; F-9 needs screen audience and looks for the report. All values are simulated and carry «Демонстрационные данные» (ТЗ §10). No cameras, no face recognition, no personal data (ТЗ §10). ТЗ §10 gives an example of one day: 8 500 people, 6 000 with gaze estimated, 2 400 looked, 40%.

Storing hourly rows costs 100 screens × 24 h × each day (72 000 rows for 30 days): over the 32 000 scan limit for one query (ADR-0004).

## Options

1. **Deterministic generator, nothing stored**: a pure function in `convex/lib/analytics.ts` returns the hourly values for (screen code, date, hour) from a hash-based seed, a per-city base level, a fixed 24-hour profile and a weekday factor. Pros: no storage, same numbers in every section and after every reset, any period works. Cons: values are computed on each read.
2. **Stored hourly rows generated by the seed**: Pros: plain reads. Cons: scan limit, large reset, fixed date window.
3. **Stored daily totals + fixed hourly profile**: Pros: smaller. Cons: still a fixed date window and reset cost.

## Decision

Option 1. The generator is written so that daily totals have a closed form (daily base × weekday factor × screen factor; hourly = daily × fixed profile share), so period sums cost screens × days, and the hourly breakdown is computed only for the F-8 chart. Vehicles are returned only when `screens.hasVehicleMetric` is true. Screens without a camera (`hasCamera` false) get no audience values: the UI shows «нет камеры» in their place, and they are left out of all audience totals (traffic, looks, contacts D-4, screen audience in the report) (OQ-3, auto). Reason: Auto: recommended answer, 2026-10-28; no estimate looks confirmed (PRODUCT.md principle 1), and «камера да / нет» stays meaningful. Age and gender shares are fixed per screen from the seed hash. Values are rounded to whole numbers. The same function serves F-2, F-3, F-4, F-8 and F-9. The front shows these values only with the «Демонстрационные данные» label and the algorithm-estimate note for age and gender.

## Consequences

- No analytics data to seed, reset or back up.
- Numbers for a screen never change between visits, which keeps the demo story stable.
- The plausibility of levels (for example the ТЗ example of 8 500 people per day) is a seed parameter; no real audience data is used (PRODUCT.md, Evidence on Hand).
- Screens without a camera show «нет камеры» and add 0 to audience totals (OQ-3, auto); the seed gives cameras to most screens (ADR-0006).
- Phase 2 with real cameras replaces the generator with stored measured data; that brings personal-data questions (docs/PERSONAL-DATA.md).
